✨ Overview

SpacePixels Additions enhances your Minecraft experience with a small collection of clean, high-quality decorative blocks and useful quality-of-life additions. The mod focuses on staying vanilla-friendly while giving builders more creative freedom.

🧩 Compatibility

The mod is currently semi-compatible with Catalogue and will be worked on for full compatibility!

🧱 Blocks & Features

This mod introduces a selection of marble-based blocks and decorative elements, all designed in a consistent 16x16 pixel style:

White Marble – clean and minimal, perfect for modern builds and interiors Red Marble – rich and vibrant, ideal for accents and luxurious structures Light Blue Marble – soft and cool-toned, great for sci-fi or fantasy themes Marble Shards (Item) – small broken pieces used for crafting or decoration Shoji Block – a Japanese-inspired paper block with a wooden grid, perfect for elegant interiors and traditional builds

All textures are designed to blend seamlessly with Minecraft while adding subtle detail and variation.
